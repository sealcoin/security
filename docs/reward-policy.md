# Reward Policy

Rewards are discretionary and determined by SEALCOIN based on severity, impact, exploitability, report quality, and remediation value.

Rewards are expressed in USD equivalent and may be paid in QAIT tokens.

## Baseline reward ranges

| Severity | Indicative USD-equivalent reward |
|---|---:|
| Informational | No reward or recognition only |
| Low | $50 – $150 |
| Medium | $150 – $500 |
| High | $500 – $2,000 |
| Critical | $2,000 – $10,000 |

## Severity guidance

Severity may be assessed using CVSS v3.1 as a reference, but SEALCOIN may adjust severity based on business logic, ecosystem impact, on-chain financial exposure, exploitability, and regulatory considerations.

| Severity | Typical CVSS range |
|---|---:|
| Informational | 0.0 |
| Low | 0.1 – 3.9 |
| Medium | 4.0 – 6.9 |
| High | 7.0 – 8.9 |
| Critical | 9.0 – 10.0 |

## Factors increasing reward amount

- Clear, reproducible steps
- High-quality Proof of Concept
- Demonstrated impact without causing harm
- Direct risk to user funds, smart contracts, platform integrity, or sensitive data
- Identification of root cause and useful remediation guidance

## Factors reducing or removing reward eligibility

- Duplicate report
- Known issue
- Out-of-scope asset
- Insufficient technical detail
- No reproducible impact
- Theoretical issue only
- Automated scanner output without validation
- Public disclosure before remediation
- Violation of responsible disclosure rules
- Legal, sanctions, tax, AML, or regulatory constraints preventing payment

## Token payment calculation

Where a reward is granted in QAIT, the number of QAIT tokens may be calculated using a reasonable market reference at the time of the reward decision or payment processing, as determined by SEALCOIN.

SEALCOIN may refuse, delay, or adjust a payment where required by law, regulation, sanctions screening, AML obligations, tax requirements, or internal compliance rules.
