# Disclosure Policy

SEALCOIN follows coordinated vulnerability disclosure for the Security Research Program.

## Default Timeline

The default disclosure window is 90 days from SEALCOIN's acknowledgement of a complete private report.

Researchers must not publicly disclose vulnerability details before remediation, mitigation, or written agreement from SEALCOIN.

## Extensions

SEALCOIN may request an extension when remediation requires smart contract migration, Bridge coordination, third-party dependency fixes, user protection measures, legal review, or regulatory handling.

Researchers may also request earlier disclosure after remediation. SEALCOIN will review reasonable requests in good faith.

## Public Advisory

SEALCOIN may publish a GitHub Security Advisory or public write-up after a fix is available, users are protected, and disclosure timing is agreed. Advisories may include severity, affected assets, impact, remediation, credit, and timeline.
