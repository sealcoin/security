# FAQ

## Should I Open a Public Issue for a Vulnerability?

No. Exploitable vulnerabilities must be reported confidentially through GitHub Private Vulnerability Reporting.

## Can I Scan Production?

Light, non-disruptive testing is allowed for in-scope assets. Do not run volumetric scans, destructive tests, spam, brute force, or tests likely to affect service availability or real users.

## Can I Exploit a Vulnerability to Prove Impact?

Use the minimum safe proof needed. Do not access, modify, delete, or exfiltrate user data. Stop testing and report immediately if you encounter sensitive data or unintended access.

## Can I Access User Data?

No. If user data is exposed, capture only the minimum evidence needed to prove the issue, stop testing, and report it privately.

## How Do I Check Whether a Vulnerability Is Already Known?

You may check public issues and advisories. Some known vulnerabilities may be private during remediation. SEALCOIN will identify duplicates during triage where possible.

## How Are Rewards Paid?

Rewards, where granted, may be paid in QAIT tokens based on a USD-equivalent amount determined by SEALCOIN. Payment may require legal, sanctions, AML, tax, or regulatory checks.

## Are SEALSQ, WISeKey, WISeID, or WISeSat Assets in Scope?

No. Assets belonging only to SEALSQ, WISeKey, WISeID, WISeSat, or other affiliated companies are not covered by default, even where the companies are affiliated with SEALCOIN.

SEALCOIN may forward reports internally to the appropriate owner. Shared infrastructure is reviewed under this program only where the report creates direct impact on an explicitly in-scope SEALCOIN asset.

## Are Rewards Guaranteed?

No. Rewards are discretionary and may be refused, reduced, delayed, or adjusted by SEALCOIN.

## Can Multiple Researchers Receive Rewards?

Usually the first valid, reproducible report is eligible. SEALCOIN may consider additional credit or reward adjustments when later reports add material new impact or remediation value.

## Why Was My Report Rejected?

Common reasons include out-of-scope asset, duplicate, known issue, no reproducible impact, scanner output without validation, unsafe testing, public disclosure before remediation, or legal/compliance restrictions.

## How Do I Provide My Hedera Account ID?

After a report is accepted and a reward decision has been made, SEALCOIN may invite you to a private communication channel to exchange payment details. Do not post payment details or personal information in public GitHub issues.

## Can I Publicly Disclose My Report?

Only after remediation, mitigation, or written approval from SEALCOIN. The default coordinated disclosure window is 90 days.
