# FAQ

## Should I open a public issue for a vulnerability?

No. Exploitable vulnerabilities must be reported confidentially through GitHub Private Vulnerability Reporting.

## How do I check whether a vulnerability is already known?

You may check public issues and public advisories. Some known vulnerabilities may be private during remediation. If your report is a duplicate or already known, SEALCOIN will inform you during triage.

## How are rewards paid?

Rewards, where granted, may be paid in QAIT tokens based on a USD-equivalent amount determined by SEALCOIN.

## How do I provide my Hedera account ID?

After a report is accepted and a reward decision has been made, SEALCOIN may invite you to a private Telegram communication channel to exchange payment details.

Do not post payment details or personal information in public GitHub issues.

## Can I publicly disclose my report?

Only after remediation, mitigation, or written approval from SEALCOIN.
